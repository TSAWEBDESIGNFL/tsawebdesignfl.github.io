Welcome to a page all about our community of Tampa!

This website is home to the story of Tampa, a city central to the state of Florida with rich history within. The city is home to over 414,000 people as of 2026, but more people are moving in than ever.

To get started, check out the Tampa Timeline to see a chronological presentation of historical milestones. Visit Fun Facts to learn more about the city and the current state of Tampa. If you're thinking of visiting Tampa or Florida in general, visit Travel Tips for real recommendations on dining, hotels, attractions, and more from real Tampa residents!
