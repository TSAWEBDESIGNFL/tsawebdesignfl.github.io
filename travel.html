Travel Tips

When visiting Tampa, consider the following travel tips:

  • Check the weather forecast before traveling to plan your activities.
  • Explore the beautiful beaches along Tampa Bay.
  • Visit local attractions like the Tampa Bay History Center.
  • Try local cuisine, especially seafood and Cuban dishes.

For more detailed recommendations,have a look at this comprehensive travel list below!

Best Times to Visit


  • (December–February): Mild temperatures in the 60s–70s°F; great for escaping colder climates.

  • (June–August): Hot, humid, with frequent afternoon thunderstorms; plan early mornings for outdoor activities

Must-See Attractions


  • Tampa Riverwalk: A 2.6-mile scenic promenade along the Hillsborough River, linking parks, museums, restaurants, and cafes. Ideal for walking, cycling, or rollerblading

  • Ybor City: Historic Latin district formerly known for cigar production; explore colorful streets and Cuban cuisine

  • Busch Gardens Tampa Bay: Theme park and zoo with roller coasters, safari animal tours, and live shows

  • Florida Aquarium: Home to thousands of marine species with interactive exhibits, touch tanks, and occasional dolphin or manatee sightings

  • Hillsborough River State Park: Offers hiking, canoeing, kayaking, fishing, and camping about 10–15 minutes from downtown

  • Bayshore Boulevard: World’s longest continuous sidewalk (4.5 miles) for walking, jogging, and biking with scenic waterfront views

  • Tampa Museum of Art & Straz Center: Explore contemporary and classical art, theater, and live events

Dining Tips


  • Columbia Restaurant: Famous for Cuban cuisine, 1905 Salad, and live Flamenco dance shows for authentic local culture experience

  • Ulele & Armature Works: Brewpub with local dishes and vibrant food hall options, perfect for sampling Florida flavors

  • Goody Goody Burgers: Historic spot in Hyde Park Village for classic American bites

  • La Segunda Central Bakery: Iconic Cuban bakery known for fresh bread and pastries, a must-visit for breakfast or snacks

  • Bern’s Steakhouse: Upscale dining with extensive wine cellar and dessert room; try the “secret menu” at the bar for smaller bites

  • Local Seafood Shacks: Visit places like Oystercatchers or Shells Seafood for fresh catches and waterfront views

  • Oxford Exchange: Stylish café and bookstore offering brunch, tea, and light fare in a beautiful setting

Transportation Tips


  • Getting Around: Rent bikes or electric scooters through Coast Bike Share, use Tampa HART buses, or explore via TECO Streetcar. For water access, Pirate Water Taxi and kayak rentals are available

  • Airport: Tampa International Airport (TPA) is convenient with easy connections and nearby car rental options

  • Ride-Sharing & Taxis: Uber and Lyft operate widely in Tampa.

  • Parking: Downtown has metered parking and garages; check for event-related restrictions

  • Walking: Many attractions are walkable, especially along the Riverwalk and in Ybor City

  • Boat Rentals & Tours: Explore Tampa Bay via boat rentals or guided tours for a unique perspective of the city

  • Public Transit: Tampa HART buses cover much of the city; check routes and schedules in advance

  • Streetcar: TECO Line Streetcar connects downtown Tampa with Ybor City, offering a scenic and historic ride

  • Bike Rentals: Coast Bike Share provides convenient bike rentals for short trips around the city

  • Water Taxis: Pirate Water Taxi offers a fun way to explore Tampa's waterfront attractions

  • Car Rentals: Available at Tampa International Airport and throughout the city for more flexibility

  • Consider pre-booking rides with luxury car services for smooth transfers.

Acommodations


  • Downtown Tampa: Central location for culture, Riverwalk, and museums.

  • Ybor City: Boutique hotels with historic charm.

  • Channelside District: Modern hotels near attractions like the Florida Aquarium and Amalie Arena.

  • Clearwater Beach: Beautiful beachfront resorts and vacation rentals.

  • St. Pete Beach: Relaxing beachside hotels with stunning sunsets.

  • Westshore / Tampa Bay Harbor: Ideal for families and beach access.

  • Hyde Park: Upscale neighborhood with trendy shops and dining.

  • Seminole Heights: Quirky area with boutique inns and local eateries.

  • Carrollwood: Suburban area with comfortable hotels and easy highway access.

  • Brandon: Budget-friendly hotels with convenient access to Tampa attractions.

  • Safety Harbor: Quaint town with charming bed-and-breakfast options.

  • Recommended hotels include Hilton Tampa Downtown, Le Méridien Tampa, the Sandpearl Resort, and Tampa Marriott Waterside Hotel & Marina.

Packing Suggestions


  • Lightweight, breathable clothing for warm weather

  • Swimsuits and beachwear

  • Comfortable walking shoes and sandals

  • Light jacket or sweater for cooler evenings

  • Umbrella or raincoat for unexpected Florida weather

  • Sunscreen, sunglasses, and a hat for sun protection

  • Reusable water bottle for exploring the city
