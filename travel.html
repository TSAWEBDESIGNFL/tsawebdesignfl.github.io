Travel Tips

Tampa's Climate

Summer

Florida is best known for our summer weather. Enjoy warm temperatures from May to September, ideal for visiting the beach or having a pool day! The temperature stays below 100 degrees Fahrenheit. Always remember to stay hydrated!

Autumn

Enjoy warm temperatures slightly cooler than summertime. The temperature during autumn is ideal for taking walks or simply being outdoors for a while.

Winter

During winter, the temperatures begin to drop. For a short time from January through February, the temperature may drop to the mid-thirties or fourties. Bring heavy jackets, especially if your visit is early in the year.

Spring

The temperature during spring is, like most places, similar to autumn. You will notice the temperature gradually get warmer from March to May, ideal for spending time outdoors. Bring a light hoodie or jacket if you plan to visit Tampa in spring.



Scenic Views & Walks

Tampa Riverwalk

Located in downtown Tampa, the Tampa Riverwalk is a scenic walk along the Hillsborough River, spanning about 2.6 miles. It has an abundant amount of shops and parks to relax in. The Riverwalk is a good place to get some exercise done during your stay because there's ample space to jog or go on your bicycle.

St. Petersburg Pier

Although technically not inside Tampa, this pier is very close. Near Tampa International Airport and Sundial, there is a stretch leading to the fishing deck. You can walk and talk if you're bringing someone, or catch one of the trams to the other end. A variety of shops and restaurants are available to eat or drink at.


Entertainment

Busch Gardens Tampa Bay

If you like amusement parks, Busch Gardens is a must-visit. This theme park doubles as a zoo and rides are named after an animal. If you want a milder experience, check out Cobra's Curse. Or, if you're up to the challenge, go on Iron Gwazi – the most thrilling ride at the park.

Tampa Theatre

Opened in 1926, the Tampa Theatre hosts plays. The dome is beautifully decorated, and a wide range of shows happen daily. It is one of Tampa's historic sites that survives today, located downtown.

Straz Center for the Performing Arts

Downtown Tampa is also home to the Straz Center, a lively area for performances from music to plays. Nearby are billboards that show the hottest production, or go inside to find out more.
